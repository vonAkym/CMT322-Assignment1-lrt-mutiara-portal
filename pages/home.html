Frequently asked questions

How do I find out which roads are closed?

Open the Map, choose the day on the slider and select a red marker to see the dates and detour.

How do I report a problem?

Go to Report, choose a category, describe the issue and submit. You will receive a ticket number.

How do I check on my ticket?

Open Feedback and enter your ticket number to see its status and history.

Will my shop stay reachable?

Business access markers on the map show entrances and delivery hours during the works.
