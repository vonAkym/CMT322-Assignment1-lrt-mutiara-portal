Frequently asked questions

Where do I see what is happening today?

The numbers at the top of this page count today's road closures, ongoing works and urgent alerts. Select one to see the advisories behind it.

How do I find out which roads are closed?

Open Advisories and choose the type "Road closure", or open the Map and select a red marker to see the dates and detour.

How do I report a problem?

Go to Report, choose a category, describe the issue and submit. You will receive a ticket number.

How do I check on my ticket?

Open Feedback and enter your ticket number to see its status and history.

Will my shop stay reachable?

Business access advisories list temporary entrances and delivery hours during the works.
